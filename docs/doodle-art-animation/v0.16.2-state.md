# v0.16.2 state (2026-09-23)

The first build from the sound plan (`/mnt/project-files/sound/plan.md`, section 9, row S1): real instruments and short
musical stingers, all still synthesized in code. The plugin still ships no audio files, and today's pad is still the
default music bed. Beds with a pulse, the tempo grid and the hero motif as a score (S3) come later and will use these
instruments.

## What changed

- **Ten instruments** in `engine.js` (`INST`, also cue names on `SFX`): `marimba`, `vibes`, `musicBox`, `kalimba`,
  `celesta`, `glock`, `epiano`, `pluck`, `feltPiano`, `strings`. One note per call, in the film's key (`deg` of
  `music.tonic`, or `f` in Hz), varied per call in level, timbre and ring but never by more than 2 cents of pitch. A
  fixed `seed` repeats one exact note. Each has a `decay` setting and a few of its own (`hard`, `trem`, `vel`,
  `bright`), so someone listening can tune them. Moved from the plan's prototype (`proto_sound.js`) with small
  changes: less pitch wobble on the marimba (it was up to 4 cents), a high-pass on the electric piano (its FM put a
  slow offset near 0 Hz into the buffer), per-call variation on the strings, and a `deg`/`oct` pitch option on all.
- **Six stingers** (`STING`, also cue names): `motif`, `success`, `question`, `oops`, `reveal`, `resolve`. `g` scales
  the whole phrase. `reveal`'s swell starts `lead` s before the cue and ends exactly on it, and the bed now ducks by
  40% for 0.9 s under a `reveal` (the duck table is `CUE_DUCK` in `renderAudio`).
- **One Drop uses five of them**: the motif when the drop appears and again, ending on the tonic, under the end card;
  `oops` as the teardrop myth is crossed out; `reveal` as the camera settles on the whole route; `success` as the
  route closes; `resolve` under the end card's rule line. Two plinks it replaced were dropped.
- **Docs:** `sound.md` has an "Instruments and stingers" section and new rows in the event map; `api.md` and the
  sound designer's name list include them, and the sound designer's motif step now uses the `motif` stinger.
- **Tests:** `regression_test.py` renders every instrument across its range (60 notes, each twice) and measures the
  pitch by FFT; all must be within 5 cents. It also checks seeded repeats and per-call variation, and that every
  stinger renders sound without clipping. `acceptance_check.py` checks the engine, `VARIED` and `sound.md` list all
  sixteen names.

## How it was checked (cloud machine, 4 cores)

- Tuning: worst note 1.55 cents off (the marimba's intended wobble); every other instrument within about 1 cent.
- One Drop's audio: `cue_check` PASS (plink 19% of 52 judged events, no identical repeats); `audio_check` PASS with
  no warnings: mean −20.1 dB, peak −3.9 dB, −17.5 LUFS, loudness range 8.5 LU, no clipping.
- Static acceptance checks all pass.

## Known limits, left for later

- Nobody has listened to these in a film yet: tuning and levels are measured, taste is not. The demos in
  `/mnt/project-files/sound/demos/` (d01 instruments, d05 stingers) are the same sounds.
- Two renders of `strings` can differ by about 1e-7 (−135 dB) because WebAudio oscillators are not bit-exact
  between renders; the existing pads have the same property. Inaudible, and the test allows it.
- The drums, the rhythmic beds and the default music style are S3's, and wait on Kosta's style choice.
