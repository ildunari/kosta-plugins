## Known gaps, decided or accepted

- **Specimen films measure low by construction.** `story_brushes.js` sits at 1.23 and `story_gallery.js` at 1.48
  against the 1.5 house target. Their samples must hold still to be read, and the brushes keep grain fixed per
  seed, so sliding a specimen around would read as crawling texture — the one thing L8 forbids. Written up in
  `references/motion.md`, "Specimen sheets are the one exception"; explainer films keep the 1.5 target.
- `motion_check.py` reports `pops` at the Long Release's `through` seam (7.4 s) and `lensIn` (16.7 s). Looked at
  frame by frame: the colour field grows in over ~10 drawings and the lens opens smoothly. The block metric fires
  on any full-frame field sweep; the hard gate (`SNAP`) is clean in both `motion_check` and `speed_check`.
- The brush demo's sound has soft onsets at its `wipe` and `lensIn` seams (`audio_check` WARN, exit 0). Audio is
  not in the v0.13 ledger and redesigning a specimen film's cues was left out of this release.
- `life.flock` murmuration reads as an even cloud rather than a folding one. Accepted after one polish pass.
- `fields` hedgerows and the modern `skyline` glazing are the most mechanical drawings in `KIT.settle`.
- The engine's `bleed` transition costs ~364 ms/frame before any brushes; wash-heavy films feel it at bleed
  seams. Pre-existing, not addressed.
- `village` calls `house` internally, so its line weights are very slightly heavy at composed scale.
