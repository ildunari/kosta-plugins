# v0.16.8 state (2026-09-23)

Narrator presets. Kosta listened to all 30 of Gemini's voices, each reading the same line, plus six style directions
on the voices he liked. He kept Charon and its variations, Erinome and Leda, and asked for a warm, close female voice
and a second male voice, each with variations to pick from. This release adds them to the voice tool with guidance on
which to use for which film. A film made without narration is unchanged, and so is a narrated film whose
`vo/lines.json` already names a voice.

## What changed

- **Presets in `voice.mjs`.** Five narrators (`charon`, `orus`, `erinome`, `leda`, `pulcherrima`) times eight
  deliveries (`plain`, `british`, `professor`, `hushed`, `wry`, `lively`, `storyteller`, `intimate`). A delivery is a
  one-line direction plus the pace it really reads at, measured on the five voices.
  - `node voice.mjs presets` lists them (`--json` for all 40).
  - `lines --preset NAME` writes the preset's voice, direction and pace into `vo/lines.json`; `voice.json` records it.
  - A new Gemini film with no voice named gets its style's preset: documentary `charon`, explainer
    `charon-professor`, short-form `charon-lively`. Before, it got Charon with "Say calmly, with quiet curiosity",
    which read at about 100 words a minute against a target of 138 and was flagged on every plate.
  - `audition --presets A,B,C` compares presets; a Gemini audition with nothing named compares the five narrators
    (or their `lively` delivery for short-form).
- **Routing and guidance:** `references/voice-presets.md` (the cast, the deliveries, which preset for which request
  or film, and rotating narrators so consecutive films don't sound alike). `SKILL.md` lists it and has a rule for it;
  `voice-providers.md` points to it.

## How it was checked

- `voice_test.mjs`: 16 new checks against a pretend Gemini server (the preset table, `lines --preset`, hand edits
  kept, a style change moving a default preset, bad names and providers refused, the voice and direction actually
  sent, `@direction` still winning, both audition modes, no preset on other providers). 117 pass.
- Real Gemini takes (about 70 cents in all, recorded in the project thread): all 30 voices on one line; eight
  deliveries on Charon, Orus, Erinome, Leda and Pulcherrima (six on Sadaltager). Every take was transcribed back
  with Gemini 2.5 Flash, or its length checked against the line, and no direction was read aloud. Paces and pitches
  in the tables come from those takes.
- Pulcherrima was picked as the "warm, close" voice by measurement among the female voices Kosta had not ruled out:
  the lowest median pitch (about 135 Hz), the least regular voicing (the huskiest) and a dark spectrum. Orus was
  picked as the second male voice as the one furthest from Charon in pitch (about 150 against 118 Hz) with the
  widest pitch range. Kosta had not heard either in its final role; swapping one is a one-line change to
  `NARRATORS`.

## Known limits

- Each pace is one take per voice; takes vary by about 10%.
- Presets cover Gemini only. The other providers keep their own voices and the style's direction.
- The open PR for narration in the workflow (#20) adds `references/voice.md`; its "Providers and voices" section
  should point to `voice-presets.md` for choosing the voice once both are merged.
