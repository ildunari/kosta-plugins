# v0.15 state (2026-09-18)

Branch `doodle-v0.15`. v0.15 came from an adversarial review of "The Slow Squeeze", a film Cowork made with
v0.13 from Kosta's lab paper on pressing drug-loaded polymer tablets. All ten plates failed: text printed over
artwork at contrast as low as 1.4 while `text_check` reported CLEAN, story text at 13–16 px, a hero whose
identity changed five times and whose log ran backwards, a pressed tablet that came out the same height it went
in, and 131 identical sounds. The agreed ledger is V1–V8 in `ACCEPTANCE.md`; L1–L17 and W1–W9 still apply.

## What changed

- **Text the story needs is legible.** Every engine text call has a role — `fact` (28 px on screen), `label`
  (22, the default), `hud` (18) or `decor` (exempt) — and the components meet their floors by default, with
  darker greys (contrast ≥ 4.5). Text sits on a **glyph halo** by default: the letters are stroked in the
  plate's paper colour before they are filled, so line art is knocked back only around the letters and the
  drawing stays whole. `backing: 'card'` gives a torn scrap; `false` turns it off.
- **`legibility_check.mjs`** looks at pixels: each line's size on screen against its role, and its contrast and
  clutter in a thin ring around the glyphs, on the frame as the viewer sees it. It does not judge composition:
  readable text across a gauge face passes, and that stays the reviewer's call.
- **Layout by relationship, not coordinates** (`style.md`, "Layout: bands and clearances"): the header and
  Journey Log bands stay clear, overlap is right when text is written on its own surface, and a clash is fixed
  by moving, re-sequencing, a card, weight or colour. The furniture table no longer gives story elements pixel
  positions.
- **A story, not a tour of the source** (`writing.md`): one hero, one identity, a want, an obstacle and a change;
  a source's sections become obstacles and turning points. `script-reviewer` checks for both failures.
- **Things that are acted on change** (`animation-principles.md`), a `Changes` column in the scene script, and a
  film-reviewer fail for a key object that stays static through its action.
- **`story_check.mjs`**: elapsed time runs forward, stage numbers don't repeat, the hero keeps one ID.
- **Sound**: 14 new synthesized effects (crunch, creak, pump, relay, hiss, plop, slosh, shaker, clink, pour,
  foil, droplet, page flip, peg snap) and 4 beds (room tone, rain, wind, city hum), all computed, no audio files.
  Automatic sounds vary on every call; pen scratches play only on paper plates; `defineStory({ dynamics })` lifts
  the climax. **`cue_check.mjs`** fails a film where one effect dominates or repeats identically.
- **One scene verdict** (PASS/FAIL, evidence, reason, fix) at fixed checkpoints: a lane's own sheet, Gate 2 on the
  assembled film, then a small fix re-checked on its own chunk only.

## Measured

On The Slow Squeeze's own story, rebuilt on the v0.15 engine with no story change: legibility findings 446 → 155
(none from the engine's components; all are the story's own placement), identical sounds 131 → 0. `story_check`
still fails it — time runs backwards twice and three stages repeat — because those are facts in its script.

## Known limits

- `legibility_check` passes any haloed line on clutter by construction; only its contrast can still fail. It cannot
  see composition faults such as text across a gauge face.
- `story_check` reads the hero's ID label, not what the hero is; a hero that becomes a different thing under the
  same label is a reviewer's catch.
- Only The Long Release uses `dynamics`; the other bundled films still draw an `audio_check` loudness-range
  warning.
- Nothing listens to the new sounds: each was verified by spectrogram and measured statistics against what the real
  sound looks like.
