# v0.17.2 state (2026-09-23)

Narration in the workflow (voice-over build 4). The voice tool (`voice.mjs`), the engine's narration track, the
narration checks and the narrator presets were already in; this release teaches the commands and the reviewers to use
them. Narration stays off unless a film's request or intake asks for it, and a film without narration is planned,
built, checked and rendered exactly as before.

## What changed

- **A voice phase between the plan and the build.** New command `/doodle-art-animation:doodle-voice` (phase 2b):
  `voice.mjs lines`, `keys --test`, `generate`, `check` with retakes, `lock`, then an animatic (the film with
  placeholder plates over the real voice) to judge the pacing before anything is drawn. The order is words, then
  voice, then picture: the plates are timed to the locked voice.
- **`references/voice.md`:** when a film is narrated, the styles, writing for the ear (marks, delivery tags,
  pronunciation), pacing, the voice phase, providers, keys, what to do when a clip fails a check, and cost. Choosing
  the voice points to `references/voice-presets.md`.
- **The plan carries the narration.** Intake asks about it (default none) and records the style and narrator preset
  in `brief.md`; `writing.md` has the `## Narration` block format; `doodle-plan` writes the narration with the
  script, checks its length with `lines --preset` at no cost, auditions presets only on a close call, and hands a
  narrated film to `doodle-voice`.
- **Build, QA and render.** `doodle-build` stops if the voice is not locked and gives each lane its plates' narration
  and marks; `doodle-qa` runs `voice.mjs check`, the `--stems` render and `audio_check.py --narrated --stems`;
  `doodle-render` delivers the `.srt` captions.
- **Reviewers.** `script-reviewer` checks each plate's words against its length at the preset's pace, marks and
  pronunciation; `sound-designer` plans around the voice and keeps loud cues off spoken words (soften them, or move
  them 0.15-0.3 s into a pause); `audio-reviewer` runs the narrated checks; `film-reviewer` checks the screen against
  what is said; `seam-reviewer` checks lines that run across a seam.
- **Acceptance.** N1-N6 in `ACCEPTANCE.md`, checked statically by `acceptance_check.py`.

## How it was checked

- `acceptance_check.py`: all static checks pass, including N1-N6.
- `voice_test.mjs` passes on the merged toolkit.
- The commands and agents were read against the toolkit's real flags (`render.mjs`, `build.py`, `voice.mjs`,
  `audio_check.py`, `cue_check.mjs`) after the merge with main.

## Known limits

- No narrated film has been made end to end with these steps yet (N5's listening check, voice-over build 6).
- Two voices (a host and an expert) is planned, not built.
