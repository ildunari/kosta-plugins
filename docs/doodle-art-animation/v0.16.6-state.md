# v0.16.6 state (2026-09-23)

The third build from the sound plan (`/mnt/project-files/sound/plan.md`, section 9, row S3): the score. One tempo runs
through the whole film, scene starts land on bar lines, and the music beds keep time to it, so the pulse carries
straight through the seams. Today's pad (`classic`) is still the default: a film without `music.style` sounds and times
exactly as before. The default style for new films is still Kosta's call (plan section 8, decision 1).

## What changed

- **Styles** (`MUSIC_STYLES`): `classic` (the pad), `notebook` (marimba arpeggio, felt-piano chords, plucked bass,
  100 to 120 bpm), `kalimba` (the same on kalimba), `lofi` (e-piano chords, brushed backbeat, soft kick, swung shaker,
  vinyl crackle, 84 to 96 bpm), `calm` (phasing loops over a drone, 84 to 100 bpm).
- **Tempo grid** (`GRID`, `fitScore`, run inside `defineStory`). Without narration every plate but the last is
  lengthened to whole bars at the tempo in range that adds the least time (One Drop: 55 s becomes 57.5 to 58 s). With
  narration the tempo and the first downbeat are searched for the most plate starts on bar lines, moving tails by up
  to `music.nudge` (0.15 s); a tail only shortens if its narration still ends 0.3 s before the cut. A plate that still
  misses plays the pad in free time (`p.free`). Narrated "Salt in Water" in `notebook`: 2 of 3 seams land.
- **Beds** (`MUS`: `pad`, `arp`, `lofi`, `pulse`, `phasing`) and soft drums (`DRUM`), moved from the prototype.
- **Chord plan and tension:** I first, ii then V into the climax plate, home to I on the end card; tension rises to
  the climax and adds layers (bass 0.35, full arpeggio or drums 0.5, strings 0.8, a pulse under the plate before the
  climax). `music.chords`, `music.tension` and plate `tension`, `climax`, `music` override them.
- **The hero's motif:** `music.motif` plus plate `motif: true | 'vary' | 'resolve'`, on the first beat after the
  transition lands; the end card resolves it automatically.
- **Beat snapping:** instruments, stingers and `chime` within 40 ms of a half beat move onto it; effects do not.
  Transitions duck a rhythmic bed by 25% instead of 50%.
- **Docs:** `sound.md` "The score", `api.md`, and the sound designer now writes plate lengths in whole bars.
- **Tests:** `regression_test.py` checks classic is unchanged and that three styles land every start on a bar line,
  play and resolve the motif, rise in tension and render unclipped.

## Known limits

- One tempo per film: the plan's small tempo steps at scene changes are not built.
- No pre-lap (the next scene's music starting just before its picture), and snapped chimes are not re-pitched into key.
- The layer levels were set by measurement (peaks and RMS), not by a listening pass; a film QA pass should listen.
