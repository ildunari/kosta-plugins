# v0.17.1 state (2026-09-23)

Blueprint and Lab paper sets (paper build B). 0.17.0 made papers pluggable but shipped only the notebook. This
release adds the two sets the paper research ranked first, four single papers for one-off plates, and the planner
and reviewer steps that choose and check a paper. A film that names no paper is unchanged.

## What changed

- **Blueprint** (`grounds/blueprint.js`, `paper: 'blueprint'`).
  - `whiteprint` (light): off-white `#eeede4`, a faint non-photo-blue drafting grid, diazo developer streaks. The
    treatment tints the whole scene toward diazo blue.
  - `blueprint` (dark): Prussian-blue gradient with uneven exposure, a white grid at 8% or less, two fold creases.
    The treatment tints toward `#2d62b0` and punches a light paper tooth through the drawing.
  - Accents: warm yellow `#ffd166` on blueprint (the vermilion is only 2.7:1 there) and a red pencil `#c2412d` on
    whiteprint. `PAL.bpLine` (`#9bc4d3`) is the pale cyan of real cyanotype linework, for lines only.
- **Lab** (`grounds/lab.js`, `paper: 'lab'`).
  - `graph` (light): warm white, a teal 30 px grid with a heavier line every 150 px, a red lab-book margin rule. No
    treatment.
  - `fluorescence` (dark): near-black `#07080b` with faint out-of-focus glows. The treatment raises saturation and adds
    a bloom. On its plates `PAL.pink`, `mint`, `cyan` and `navyFill` become colour-blind-safe channel colours (also
    `FLUOR.magenta`, `green`, `cyan`, `dapi`), with DAPI lifted to `#4f7bff`.
  - The `engineering` set pairs `graphgreen` with fluorescence.
- **Single papers for one plate** (`plate.paper = '…'`): `graphgreen` (engineering pad), `dotgrid`, `hexpaper`
  (hexagon lattice) and `semilog` (three log decades, for release and clearance curves).
- **`KIT.lab.scaleBar`**: a microscope scale bar for fluorescence plates.
- **Header sounds** come from 0.16.6's `PAPER_PEN` table (technical pen on the blueprint set, pencil on the grids,
  readout blips on fluorescence). `graphgreen` and `hexpaper` are not in that table and name `pencil` themselves.
- **Workflow.** `intake.md` and `doodle-plan` now have the planner pick the paper set from the topic and write it as
  the first line of `script.md` (`Paper: <set> (why)`), asking the user only when the look is a matter of taste.
  `film-reviewer` checks that subjects read on the chosen paper, that colour alone carries no meaning on the tinted
  blueprint papers, and that a film keeps to one set. `style.md` gains "Choosing the paper set" with the palettes,
  contrast figures and defaults.

## How it was checked

- One Drop built on `blueprint`, `lab` and `engineering`, plus a variant with a `hexpaper` plate and a scale bar,
  and stills rendered from light and dark plates. No page errors.
- Contrast of every palette against its paper: ink 7.6–17.2:1, labels 5.7–10.0:1, accents 4.0–7.7:1, all above the
  floors (7, 4.5, 3). The smoke test's paper probe (`__paperProbe` on `story_swatch.js`) passes on every paper.
- The header sound each plate uses was read back from the built films (`penOf`): techPen on both blueprint pages,
  pencil on graph, graphgreen and hexpaper, readout on fluorescence.
- `regression_test.py`, `effects_test.py` and `acceptance_check.py` pass locally (the audio check that needs numpy and
  ffmpeg runs in CI); the smoke workflow is green.

## Known limits

- **Render cost.** Treated papers are slower in headless Chromium with no GPU (4-core thread VM, median of 10 warm
  frames): blueprint and whiteprint plates cost about 90–140 ms more per frame than the notebook, fluorescence about
  150–225 ms more, graph paper nothing measurable. A 90 s film made entirely of treated plates adds roughly 1.5–2
  minutes of render time with 4 workers.
- The blueprint tint removes hue from everything inside the scene (the HUD keeps its colours), so two subjects told
  apart only by colour look alike there. `style.md` and `film-reviewer` say so; nothing enforces it.
- The whiteprint's glyph halos can read as a faint pale patch behind a label over dense hatching.
- No film has been made on these papers yet; the first one should get a look at its contact sheet before the render.
