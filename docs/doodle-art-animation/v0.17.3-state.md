# v0.17.3 state (2026-09-23)

Chalk, Codex and Toned papers: build step C of the canvases plan (`/mnt/project-files/canvases/plan.md`, sections
4–5). It adds three paper sets on top of the 0.17.0 paper registry, each as files in `toolkit/grounds/`, plus a
board-eraser transition and a minimum line width for chalk.

It follows 0.17.2 (0.17.1 is the Blueprint and Lab papers, build B; 0.17.2 is narration in the film workflow).

## What changed

- **Three paper sets.** `defineStory({ paper: 'chalk' | 'codex' | 'toned' })` puts light plates on the first paper
  and dark plates on the second:

  | Set | Light paper | Dark paper |
  |---|---|---|
  | `chalk` | `whiteboard`: glossy white, erased-marker ghosts | `chalkboard`: dark green slate, eraser smears, chalk dust |
  | `codex` | `laid`: Leonardo-style laid paper, foxing, tide rings | `staratlas`: navy with stars, a Milky Way band and a turning coordinate net |
  | `toned` | `kraft`: brown kraft paper | `sketchbook`: warm black paper |

  A lighter `tan` paper (not in a set) suits text-heavy plates; a plate names it with `paper: 'tan'`.
- **Accents.** Kraft and tan get a dark red accent, because the standard vermilion is 1.6:1 on kraft. Each paper's
  accent, ink and label colours clear the contrast floors (ink 7, label 4.5, accent 3), measured against its texture.
- **Chalk lift.** The chalkboard's treatment takes the difference between the plate and the bare board, punches the
  board's tooth through it and adds it back onto the board. Pale ink turns chalk white, dark fills turn pale chalk of
  their own colour, and the board itself stays untouched.
- **`stroke: { min, from }` on a paper.** `pen()` and `ink()` lines of `from` px or more are drawn at least `min` px
  wide on that paper. The chalkboard sets `{ min: 5, from: 2.5 }`; the notebook papers set nothing, so their frames
  are unchanged.
- **`erase` transition.** A felt board eraser wipes the old plate away in overlapping zigzag columns and the new plate
  shows through the wiped path (`enter: { type: 'erase', dir: 'rl' }` runs it right to left). Default 1.4 s, at
  least 1.2 s. Its sound is the eraser effect from 0.16.6. It is in the reel.
- **Header sounds** match the 0.16.6 `PAPER_PEN` table: marker, chalk, quill, charcoal and pen scratch.
- **Faster tooth.** `treat()`'s tooth draws one cached, frame-sized hole mask (`toothMask`) instead of filling the
  frame with a scaled pattern; the chalkboard uses the same mask.
- **Tests.** The swatch check in `regression_test.py` finds the fixture and notebook plates by paper name, since
  every grounds file adds sets to the swatch.
- **Docs:** `style.md` (the sets, per-paper rules, render cost), `api.md` (`stroke`), `motion.md`, `sound.md`,
  `writing.md`, `SKILL.md` and the sound designer.

## How it was checked

- Every new paper's ink, label and accent contrast, measured on the swatch against its texture (lowest: laid accent
  3.33, kraft accent 3.55).
- `smoke_test.py`: all stories and the MP4 segment pass. `regression_test.py` passes (56 checks after merging 0.16.7).
- `speed_check` passes on the reel's erase seam.
- CI (`doodle-smoke`) green on the branch before each merge.

## Known limits

- Treated papers cost render time on the cloud VMs, which have no GPU: roughly 20–100 ms more per frame at 1080p,
  the chalkboard most and the whiteboard nothing extra (`style.md` has the table).
- The star atlas's coordinate net replaces the topographic contour loops, so a plate on it has no contours.
- `tan` is a single paper, not part of a set.
