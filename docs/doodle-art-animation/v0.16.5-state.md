# v0.16.5 state (2026-09-23)

Voice-over build 3: checks on the narration. Nothing changes a film made without narration, and the step commands
and reviewers do not ask for a voice yet (build 4 switches narration on). The voice tool (PR #14) and the engine's
narration track (PR #15) merged without a version of their own and first shipped in 0.16.3 (see v0.16.3-state.md
for build 5, the extra providers). The plan these builds follow is the project's `voiceover/plan.md`.

## What changed

- **Voice checks (PR #21).**
  - `voice_check.py` transcribes each clip (Gemini through the proxy, faster-whisper, or a fixed file for tests)
    and compares it with the script: a missing word fails, a direction read aloud fails as extra words. It flags
    a take whose pitch or brightness is far from the film's other clips, and `--listen` asks Gemini Flash to score
    the delivery against its direction. `voice.mjs check` runs it.
  - `audio_check --stems` adds `masked`: the stretches where the music and effects come within 10 dB of the voice
    while it speaks, which the median `under` figure hides.
  - `cue_check` adds `SPEECH`: a loud cue or a seam's sound that lands on a spoken word.

## How it was checked

- `voice_check_test.py` (new, in CI): a fake-voice film with a planted missing word, a direction read aloud, a
  wrong-speed clip, a silent clip and another person's voice; each is caught and the clean clip passes. A pretend
  Gemini server answers the transcript and listening requests.
- `narration_test.py`: on the narrated example, `masked` names the stretches at the two chimes (15.2-16.0 s and
  34.8-35.8 s), and `cue_check` flags the same chimes, the foil and plop on "drop" and the peg snap on "pull", and
  leaves the soft plink on "gone" alone.
- Real run in a cloud thread: five Gemini TTS clips (about $0.02) had their transcripts match the script with no
  false flags; the listening pass scored two of them 4 and 5 out of 5 for about $0.002.

## Known limits

- The faster-whisper transcriber is written but has not been run.
- The narrated example's own cues trip the new `SPEECH` and `masked` warnings. They are warnings, and the example
  was left as it is; build 4's sound-designer changes are the place to soften cues on words.
- The voice consistency check uses median pitch and spectral centroid only. It catches a clearly different voice,
  not a subtle change of timbre.
