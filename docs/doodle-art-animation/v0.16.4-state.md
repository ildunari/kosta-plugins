# v0.16.4 state (2026-09-23)

Sound build S2 from the sound plan (`/mnt/project-files/sound/plan.md`, section 9): more ambiences, more effects, and a
writing sound for each paper. The sounds come from the plan's prototype (`proto_sound.js`), tidied, seeded like the
v0.15 sounds and level-matched against the existing ones. The plugin still ships no audio files.

## What changed

- **Eight ambience beds** in `BED`: `body` (a slow heartbeat and blood rushing with it), `underwater`, `forest`,
  `ocean`, `fire`, `clockRoom` (a quiet room with a clock, for time passing), `micro` (slow viscous low sound and
  glassy tones in the film's key, for scenes inside a cell or mucus) and `vinyl` (a quiet crackle layer).
- **Eighteen effects** in `SFX`: `typewriter`, `eraser`, `stamp`, `tear`; `counter` (a tally counter under a
  count-up), `sonify` (a tone whose pitch follows a graph as it draws), `sparkle`; `pipette`, `centrifuge`,
  `syringe`, `pills`, `fizz`, `bubbles`, `squelch`, `heartbeat`, `beep`; `zap`, `magnet`. All vary per call.
- **Six writing tools**: `pencil`, `chalk`, `marker`, `quill`, `charcoal`, `techPen`. Each takes `{ chars, cps }` to
  type a line or `{ dur }` to draw one.
- **The header writes with its paper's tool.** `penOf(plate)` reads the paper registry's `sfx: { header }` (the
  paper sets foundation, when present) and otherwise the `PAPER_PEN` table: pencil on graph paper, chalk on a
  chalkboard, a marker on a whiteboard, a quill on parchment, charcoal on kraft, a technical pen on blueprint, readout
  blips where there is no pen. `plate.pen` still overrides it and now also takes a sound name. Notebook films type on
  exactly as before: the example film's `cue_check` report is the same on 0.16.3 and 0.16.4.
- **`cue_check`** warns about any writing cue (not only `scratch`) on a paper with no pen.
- Docs: `sound.md` (event map rows, the catalogue, the paper-to-tool table), `api.md`, the sound-designer agent.

## How it was checked

- Levels, with ffmpeg's EBU R128 meter on each sound rendered alone through the engine's master chain. Typed, every
  writing tool is within 0.3 LU of the pen scratch (about -34 LUFS). The effects sit between -20 and -29 LUFS, the
  range of the existing ones (pop -30, chime -19). The beds sit between -25 and -31 LUFS like the existing four,
  except `vinyl`, a layer at -35. Measured, not listened to.
- `tests/doodle-art-animation/effects_test.py` (new, run by CI) renders `fixtures/story_sounds.js`, one of each new
  sound, and checks that each exists and varies, that the paper lookup gives the right tool, that two renders agree
  within 1e-4, that nothing clips, that every cue and bed can be heard, and that the fixture passes `cue_check`.
- Chromium's offline audio is not bit-exact between two renders of the same film: main's own engine differs by up to
  about 1e-6 on One Drop. The test compares with a tolerance for that reason.

## Known limits

- Taste is untested: the sounds were checked by measurement only.
- Papers other than the notebook's two need `sfx: { header }` set in their ground files (the paper sets B and C
  builds), or the `PAPER_PEN` table covers them only when no ground is defined. The foundation's `defineGround`
  defaults every paper to `scratch` or `readout`.
- A paper does not yet choose a default ambience; the sound designer picks beds per scene.
