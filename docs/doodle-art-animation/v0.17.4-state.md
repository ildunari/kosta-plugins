# v0.17.4 state (2026-09-23)

Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS becomes the default narration voice model. Google released `gemini-3.8-flash-tts` and
`gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts` as stable models on 2026-09-22. Until the end of 2026, 3.8 Flash costs about half of
what `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` does ($9 against $20 per million audio tokens), and it acts directions and tags
more expressively. It is not a model-name swap: 3.8 reads everything it is sent as the words to speak, so a direction
or a `[tag]` sent the old way is read aloud. A film without narration is unaffected.

## What changed

- **`voice.mjs` sends Gemini 3.8 a new kind of request.** It goes to the Interactions API
  (`POST /v1beta/interactions`) with `store: false`, so Google keeps no copy. The words go alone; the preset's
  direction goes as the voice's style (`speech_metadata`), with the leading "Say" and the trailing colon removed.
- **Tags are rewritten for 3.8.** `[short pause]` and `[medium pause]` become `<short pause>` (3.8 has no medium
  pause), `[long pause]` becomes `<long pause>`, and a documented vocal sound (`[sigh]`, `[breath]`, `[laugh]` ...)
  becomes `<sigh>` and so on. A mood tag such as `[curious]` starts a new part of the same request, whose style is
  the direction plus that mood. A pause tag right before a mood tag is dropped, because the step to a new part is
  already a pause of about a second.
- **3.1 still works.** A model whose name starts with `gemini-3.1-` or `gemini-2.5-` gets the old request (one
  prompt, direction first, tags in square brackets). A film whose `vo/lines.json` already names 3.1 stays on it; it
  moves only with `--model gemini-3.8-flash-tts` on `lines`, which makes every clip again.
- **Prices and cost.** `voice.mjs` knows the prices of both 3.8 models and that they double on 2027-01-01, and
  estimates Gemini's cost at 33 audio tokens per second (measured; the docs say 25).
- **Preset paces measured again on 3.8.** 3.8 reads the same directions faster, most of all the slow ones. Each
  delivery keeps both paces, and `voice.mjs` uses the one for the film's model in `lines`, the pace check and
  `presets`: plain 125 (120 on 3.1), british 135 (120), professor 140 (120), hushed 120 (115), wry 135 (120),
  lively 185 (160), storyteller 130 (105), intimate 130 (110) words a minute. `presets` shows both columns.
- **The transcript check names what was read aloud.** `voice_check.py` fails a clip whose extra words are the
  direction or a delivery tag, and says which ("the tag [short pause] was read aloud"), instead of only counting
  extra words.
- **Docs.** `references/voice.md`, `voice-providers.md` (a new Gemini note), `voice-presets.md` (both paces) and
  `doodle-voice/SKILL.md` name the new default, the tag rewriting and the 3.1 fallback.

## How it was checked

- `voice_test.mjs`: the Gemini tests were rewritten for the new request (words alone, style, tags, mood parts,
  `store: false`), plus the 3.1 fallback, the per-model paces and the 2027 price change. `voice_check_test.py` has
  cases for a direction and a tag read aloud.
- Real calls on `gemini-3.8-flash-tts`: a plate with pause, vocal and mood tags was generated and transcribed, with
  no missing words, no extra words and no tag read aloud. This found the pause-before-mood gap (2.4 s), which is now
  fixed. An audition on two presets passed its checks.
- All 40 presets (5 voices x 8 deliveries) were generated on 3.8 reading the same 24-word line to measure the paces
  above. The recordings are in `/mnt/project-files/voiceover/samples-3.8/`.
- `acceptance_check.py` static checks pass.
- About $0.22 was spent on real calls.

## Known limits

- The slow deliveries are no longer much slower than `plain` on 3.8. Adding "speaking slowly" to the direction
  brought `storyteller` to 117 and `intimate` to 115 words a minute in a test (`samples-3.8/slower/`); the presets
  were left unchanged until Kosta has listened.
- A mood tag in the middle of a sentence adds a pause of about a second, because it starts a new part.
- Films already narrated on 3.1 stay on 3.1. Their clips are cached, and re-voicing one on 3.8 means new takes and
  a new lock.
- `gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts` takes the same request but has not been auditioned on the presets.
