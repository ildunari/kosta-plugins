# v0.16.1 state (2026-09-23)

A fix release. Every item is a gap the second film hit ("Dressed in Mucus", from Azagury et al., Small 2022, made in
a cloud session on 2026-09-22). Each has a check in `tests/doodle-art-animation/regression_test.py`, which CI runs
next to the smoke test, and each check fails on v0.16.0.

## What changed

- **`smoke_test.py` skips story parts.** A film folder built the `doodle-build` way holds `story_tail.js` (the
  `defineStory` call alone), which matched `story*.js` and failed with `P0 is not defined`. Files named
  `story_tail.js`, files `assemble.sh` concatenates, and files whose whole text sits inside another story are now
  skipped and listed; `story.js` still covers them. `--stories` can still name one.
- **A plate cannot undo its own fade.** Plates often write `ctx.globalAlpha = x` and later `ctx.globalAlpha = 1`.
  Inside a transition that threw the fade away: in the film, the title plate's ring stayed at full strength through
  the zoom into plate I and then vanished in one drawing. While `draw` and `overlay` run, `globalAlpha` now counts
  from the alpha the plate was handed (`relAlpha` in `engine.js`: reading gives 1 at the start, writing `x` sets
  `x` times the fade). Outside a fade nothing changes. Transition frames of The Long Release and One Drop are
  pixel-identical to v0.16.0, and their draw times are the same within noise (median 115–120 ms against 117–131 ms
  per transition frame on a 4-core cloud machine).
- **`zoom` dives in by default.** Before, a zoom with no `dir` zoomed out, so a script that said "zoom into the
  wall" needed `dir: 'in'`, and `motion.md` never said which way was the default. It now goes in unless the enter
  says `dir: 'out'`. The gallery's one zoom that relied on the old default now says `dir: 'out'`, so it looks the
  same. An older film folder with a bare `zoom` changes direction if it is rebuilt on 0.16.1.
- **`speed_check` warns `TWICE`.** A whip pan shows both sheets at once, so a hero still in frame on the old sheet
  while the new sheet's hero slides in is seen twice. In the film the hero was a 187 px coated ball on one side and
  a 13 px dot on the other, and read as two particles. The pan's `__seamProbe` now returns both heroes' screen
  positions and reticle sizes; `speed_check` prints a `TWICE` line (with the size jump when it is 2x or more) when
  both are in frame for three drawings or more. It is a warning and does not change the exit code. `motion.md` and
  the script reviewer now say a pan keeps one scale.
- **`audio_check` passes the engine's soft seams.** The engine builds a seam without a sharp attack: the riser
  stops, the bed ducks and the new plate's pad rises from zero. The onset test (a 50 ms window 6 dB above the last
  250 ms) missed that and warned on five of the film's six seams. A seam now also passes when the level climbs 6 dB
  or more within half a second, starting in its first 0.3 s. On the film all six pass; a flat bed still warns.
- **The script reviewer and sound designer get only what they need.** On Opus, the script reviewer was stopped by
  an automated safety check (`[bio]`) part-way through reading the full biomedical paper and its figure pages.
  `doodle-plan` now hands it `facts.md` and the source's path for spot-checks only, and the agent reads just the
  cited passage when a number looks wrong. The sound designer designs from the script alone and never opens the
  sources. Both prompts now say the films are educational explainers. This makes a stop less likely but cannot rule
  one out; the plan skill says to report one plainly if it happens.

## Known limits, left for later

- For `zoom` with `dir: 'out'`, the old plate still leans in (`LEAD.zoom` 1.06) before shrinking, a small reversal
  at the hand-off. Fixing it changes One Drop's zoom, so it was left out of a fix release.
- `TWICE` uses the reticle radius (`hero(t).r`, 34 when unset) as the hero's size. A story whose reticle does not
  match the drawn hero gets a less accurate size jump, and a hero drawn much larger than its reticle can leave
  frame later than the check thinks.
